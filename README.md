## 💫 About Me

👋 Hi! I'm Daehwan Yeo, a Computer Science Honours student at Curtin University in Western Australia.

My current focus is AI, computer vision, and high-performance computing. For my Honours research, I’m working on 3D medical image segmentation of coronary arteries from CT angiography data.

I’ve built a strong foundation in programming and software development through academic projects, personal projects, and research experience. My experience spans Python, C, Java, full-stack web development, machine learning, and HPC workflow automation.

I’m especially interested in building AI-driven systems that are practical, reliable, and useful in real-world settings — from medical imaging to scientific computing and workflow automation.

If you're working on something exciting, looking for a collaborator, or just want to chat about AI, computer vision, HPC, or software engineering, feel free to reach out! 😄
